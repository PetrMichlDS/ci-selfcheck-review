How this result is calculated

Each answer describes a situation and is worth 0 to 4 points. "I'm not sure" does not count against you: the question is left out and shows up as an open question for a colleague. A level needs at least 8 answers and at least one in every area. We add up the points you earned and compare them with the maximum for the questions answered. Your level is the share you reach: under 25% is Level 1, 25 to 44% Level 2, 45 to 64% Level 3, 65 to 84% Level 4, 85% and above Level 5. The strongest and weakest area are the highest and lowest shares among the five areas. Each area is shown as Not yet in place (under 40% of its points), Partly in place (40 to 79%) or Well in place (80% and above). The levels are our judgement, not an industry benchmark. This is a self-assessment: it shows how people describe their operation, not what an audit would find.
