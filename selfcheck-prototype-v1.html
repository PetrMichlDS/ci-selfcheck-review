How this result is calculated

Each answer describes a situation and is worth 0 to 4 points. "I'm not sure" is worth 0, because not knowing is itself a finding. For each area we add up your points and compare them with the maximum. Below 40%: Red. 40 to 79%: Amber. 80% and above: Green. The headline profile does not come from one total. It comes from the pattern across areas: how many answers were "not sure", whether customer signals are ahead of or behind quality, evidence and coaching, and whether everything is high or low. A well-run sample with a clear selection logic and regular calibration lands in Amber, not Red. The bands and profile rules are our judgement, not an industry benchmark. This is a self-assessment: it shows how people describe their operation, not what an audit would find.
