Comms Inspector self-check: three sets of questions

Working prototype of the self-check quiz for the case-study page. Nothing here is sent anywhere. Please open the sets, answer them the way you would at your own company, and tell us which questions felt clear, interesting or pointless.

How to try it. Each link opens one phase with ready-made demo data. The colleague flow (the message with a link to a colleague) stores answers in your own browser, so a link only works in the browser where it was made. Use the demo links to see every phase. Sets A, B and C keep their data separately.

Questions B: after feedback (12 questions)

Same screens and scoring. What differs is the questions:

  • New first question: which customer channels the review covers (calls, chats, emails, bots). The share of reviewed conversations now refers to those channels.
  • New: what is prepared for the agent before a call; whether signals are pulled out of conversations automatically.
  • Feedback to agents has a new best answer: during the conversation.
  • Reworded: what the agent promised on a call (instead of a required statement); how other teams get insights.
  • Removed: how reviewed conversations are chosen; how long a complaint pattern takes to reach a team; how required steps are checked.

Questions C: the short version (8 questions)

Same screens, same scoring idea, but only eight questions. It is meant to be answered faster, with the aim that more people finish. Which questions stayed:

  • Channels covered, and the share of conversations reviewed
  • What is prepared for the agent before a call, and how soon the agent gets feedback
  • Whether reviewers score the same conversation alike (calibration)
  • How long it takes to find out what the agent promised
  • Whether signals are pulled out of conversations automatically, and how other teams get insights

Removed from B: whether coaching worked, how quality criteria are defined, what happens to a life event a customer mentions, and whether anything changed because of what customers said.

What is different in the rules. A level now needs at least 6 answers (it was 8), spread over at least four of the five areas. Quality and Evidence have only one question each, so an “I'm not sure” there leaves the area empty. With one area empty the level is shown as provisional and leaves that area out; with two or more empty it is not shown until a colleague answers. The time “about 4 minutes” is an estimate in proportion to the 12-question version, not a measured time. The extra five questions (below) are answered only by the person who fills in the quiz; colleagues still get only the first eight.

Sharpen your result (optional, after the result): five more questions that the first eight do not reach.

  • How would you find a conversation that went badly, if the customer never complained?
  • Which conversations end up being reviewed? (how they are chosen)
  • Do you check whether coaching worked? (brought back from the original 12)
  • Do you check whether high quality scores go with satisfied customers?
  • Where do your records of why customers contact you come from?
